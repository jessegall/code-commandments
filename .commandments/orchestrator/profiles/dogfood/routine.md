# routine

<!-- what you do EVERY time you come to a stop — the standing habits, nudged not enforced -->

**Told the workflows agent?** Every commit that has been pushed, and every ruling you
made on something they raised. They cannot see this tree. And only ever AFTER the
push — a peer pointed at a version they cannot install goes looking for it, and its
probes then test the old binary.

**Anything the far side of a compaction would need?** A ruling the user gave once,
an approach you abandoned, the reason behind the work in hand. Say it with
`journal remember`, not in a sentence that dies with the summary.

**Is a worker waiting on you?** A report is a decision nobody has made yet. Accept,
rework, or say what you are waiting for — but do not leave it reported.

**Is the record what you would say out loud?** If the board says `working` and the
work is finished, or a green covers a tree that has moved, fix the record before you
stop rather than carrying the difference in your head.
