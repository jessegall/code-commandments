# journal-enforced

{quiet} tool calls and nothing recorded. {tagged} tag(s) this session. Whatever you have
decided in that stretch is now only in your head, and a compaction takes exactly that.

Record ONE line before the next call — and record the RIGHT thing. Tag the message you
are already writing: open a line with [!discovery], [!correction] or [!update]. That is
free, and it is what clears this.

A pin is not the cheap way out. `{binary} journal remember "<the fact>"` is for a ruling,
a constraint, or a decision and its reason — never a status, a count, or what you just
did. Only the last twelve pins reach a compaction, so a needless one pushes a real one
out.

It clears the moment anything is filed.
